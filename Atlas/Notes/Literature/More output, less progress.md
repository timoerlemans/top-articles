# More output, less progress?

Bron: Anton from Manager.dev · mailto:reader-forwarded-email/c7dea8cc79c598c2e90f628ada0509e5

## Highlights

> As an industry, we are building a lot of great tools to align agents. There are new harnesses coming out every week. But we don’t seem to be spending much time discussing how to better align people in the new context. And the people are the ones driving the agents . When each of them can produce ten times more, any misalignment between them gets amplified too.
