# It’s Time to Take Starlink Away from Elon Musk

Bron: Paul Krugman · mailto:reader-forwarded-email/d9db0e4e5ce318b6afda5331af9b1c58

## Highlights

> Did Elon Musk decide by personal fiat to cut off Ukraine’s access to vital communications? Although Musk claims that the decision was made by Trump, according to the Wall Street Journal, some U.S. officials state that Musk did indeed take it upon himself to pull the plug on Ukraine. Secretary of State Marco Rubio is quoted as saying that the administration was blind-sided: “There was no foreknowledge.”
