# Freud’s Id, Ego, and Superego: Who Is Really in Control of You?

Bron: Philosophy Stuff from Philosophy Stuff · https://read.readwise.io/read/01m613n2rr9bsvc06wyrrzr52s

## Highlights

> Between them sits the ego , the part of the self that deals with reality. It must satisfy the id’s demands in ways the world will permit, and it must answer to the superego’s standards. Freud described it as a poor creature serving three masters, the id, the superego, and the outside world, and threatened by danger from all three.
